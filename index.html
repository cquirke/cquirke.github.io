SRAD H-Class Solid Rocket Motor

38mm RNX-71: Design Through Manufacturing

Led design of a student-researched-and-developed 38mm H-class motor, the team's first, targeting 400 psi chamber pressure. Sized throat and grain geometry by hand and in MATLAB, cross-validated performance in OpenRocket and RPA, and ran thermal/fluid analysis in ANSYS. Designed a two-part aluminum/phenolic-insert conical nozzle for manual-lathe manufacturability. Case and closure manufacturing began before the project was paused by the university for review; development continues independently.

SRM Design Automation Tool

MATLAB GUI: Analytical Solid Motor Sizing

Built a MATLAB GUI that walks through the full solid motor design process analytically: sets operating pressure from chamber size or target pressure, sizes the throat for minimal grain surface area at that pressure, optimizes grain geometry across multiple pattern types, calculates nozzle contour or conical geometry based on manufacturing method, and sizes closure retention, including bolt pattern/thread sizing based on user input. Currently validating outputs against known references and hand calculations.

L2 Certification Rocket

Dual-Motor Cert Build: Design, Failure Analysis, and Rebuild

Built and flew a minimal-diameter Blue Tube airframe rocket to certify Level 1 (H550) and Level 2 (J425) with a 36" main on a chute-release/ripcord dual-deploy setup, no onboard avionics. L1 flew clean with a good recovery. L2 coned near apogee, likely a combination of reduced stability margin at low velocity and wind gusts at apogee, then suffered a deployment failure when a recovery knot failed, compounded by ripcord zippering leading to a week-long recovery and a warped aft airframe. Currently rebuilding: replacing the damaged aft section with an added lower coupler for structural margin, and designing a low-cost custom flight computer PCB for real deployment data ahead of re-flight.
